What this tracker is for

A shared, living record of every EVO router that comes back from the field — why it was swapped, whether the market manager vetted it, and what ultimately happened to it. It follows each unit through the whole chain so the warehouse can reconcile returns and everyone has clear visibility on which routers went back to Evolution Digital and which did not.

1 · Swapped in field→ 2 · Vetted by market manager→ 3 · Delivered to warehouse→ 4 · Sent to Evolution as defective (or kept/scrapped)

How to use it: add a unit when it comes back (link it to an existing swap if there is one, or log it standalone). Managers record their vetting + findings; warehouse records what was received and the final disposition. Update an entry as it moves along — every change is saved and audit-logged.
